About the app

Plan your trips, powered by AI

Wayra is a travel planning app for iOS that helps you organize every aspect of your trips — from generating AI-powered day-by-day itineraries to managing packing lists and storing travel documents.

Whether you're planning a weekend getaway or a multi-week adventure, Wayra keeps everything in one place and syncs securely across your devices.

Data & Privacy

How we use your data

GOOGLE SIGN-IN

Why we request Google account access

Wayra uses Google Sign-In as an optional, convenient way to create and access your account. We request only your basic profile information — your name and email address — to identify your account and personalize your experience. We do not access your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or any other Google services. Your Google credentials are never stored by Wayra directly; authentication is handled securely via Supabase.

ACCOUNT DATA

Name & email address

Used to create and identify your Wayra account. Your email is used to send account-related notifications (password reset, etc.) and is never shared with third parties or used for marketing without your consent.

APP CONTENT

Trip data

Your trips, itineraries, packing lists, and uploaded documents are stored securely in the cloud and synced to your devices. This data belongs to you and is only accessible by your account. You can delete all your data at any time from the app settings.

NO SELLING

We do not sell your data

Wayra does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
